# Customer Expansion Playbook

> Replit expansion: current usage, opportunities, qualification, and readiness signals

This playbook uses **Replit** as the selected customer. Replit migrated from Docusaurus to Mintlify and gets measurable value from docs-as-code, collaboration, and automation. This page discusses the account's current usage, prioritized expansion opportunities, potential barriers to adoption, qualification criteria, and signals of expansion readiness.

## How Replit uses Mintlify today

Replit moved its docs from Docusaurus to Mintlify and runs a Git-backed, version-controlled workflow so anyone on the team can contribute. Contributors grew **from 2 to 10+**; each PR **saves \~30–60 minutes**, and sales report that customers find information easily via search. Replit uses Vale linting, automated changelogs (**\~90% reduction in manual effort**), and AI-assisted writing. Docs are organized by type (tutorials, how-tos, reference). Replit Agent and other AI features are documented on [docs.replit.com](https://docs.replit.com), which also exposes an LLM-friendly documentation index.

***

## Expansion opportunities

* **AI Assistant**: Replit serves beginners to professionals with high how-to volume. Mintlify's AI assistant resolves a high share of documentation queries without human intervention and improves developer satisfaction, allowing for high deflection and cost savings.
* **Analytics for AI/agent traffic and Assistant usage**: Mintlify offers [analytics for AI and agent traffic](https://www.mintlify.com/blog/agent-analytics) (which agents visit, which pages they hit, MCP search) and [Assistant analytics](https://mintlify.com/docs/guides/analytics) (conversations, cited sources, categories). Replit invests heavily in Replit Agent and documents it on Mintlify; they are a strong fit for using these analytics to see how docs support agent and human usage and to surface content gaps.
* **Enterprise / RBAC / multi-site admin**: With 10+ contributors, admin controls (RBAC, audit logs, multi-site) reduce friction and support further seat growth.
* **AI translations**: If a meaningful share of Replit's user base is non-English, AI-powered doc translations align with making software creation universally accessible.

***

## Why adoption hasn't happened yet

Adoption can lag for several reasons: post-migration focus (consolidating the new workflow), ownership gaps (no clear owner for the next phase), or scope (content and accessibility prioritized over new product features). Below are the main factors for Replit.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="check-double" title="Post-migration focus">
    The team is likely in an "it works, don't touch it" phase after the Docusaurus→Mintlify move. Expanding from 2 to 10+ contributors is a major change to absorb before taking on more product features.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="user" title="Ownership gap">
    The engineering effort that ran the migration may be complete; there may be no single owner yet for the next phase (e.g. AI features on docs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="list" title="Content and accessibility first">
    The [blog](https://www.mintlify.com/blog/behind-replits-documentation-transformation) emphasizes rewriting for "vibe coders" and agents and improving processes. User-facing AI chat on docs may not have been in scope.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Signals of expansion readiness

* **Onboarding push**: Public focus on improving onboarding (docs and AI are enablers).
* **International growth**: Localization or non-English user growth (opens AI translations).
* **New docs/DevRel hire**: Dedicated owner often means appetite for new features.
* **Repeat questions in docs**: Forum/support questions already answered in docs (AI assistant would deflect).
* **High search failure rate**: Mintlify analytics show poor findability; bring data to the conversation.
* **High AI/agent traffic or Assistant citation issues**: Sustained agent traffic or Assistant conversations with poor citations; creates a natural fit for Mintlify's analytics for AI/agent traffic and Assistant usage.
* **Playground/playbook requests**: Asks for capacity, streaming, or agent playbooks (paid capacity or pro services).
